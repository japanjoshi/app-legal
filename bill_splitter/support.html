SplitSnap: Support

SplitSnap splits a bill between friends and sends each one their own receipt with a UPI QR for their share. It is published by 1213 Studios. The answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? Email japanjoshi1234@gmail.com with your phone model and what you were trying to do. We usually reply within two working days.

Friends can't pay from the QR

  • Open Settings → How friends pay you and check your UPI ID is spelled exactly as it is in your UPI app, for example name@okaxis. A wrong ID makes the payment fail in your friend's app.
  • A QR can't be scanned from the phone it is shown on. A friend who only has the receipt on their own phone can save the image and choose it from the gallery in GPay, PhonePe or Paytm.

A scanned bill came out wrong

Scanning reads the photo on your phone, so a clear, flat, well-lit photo of the whole bill works best. You can correct any line, the tax or the total on the bill screen before splitting, or type the bill in yourself.

How many bills can I scan?

Three scans a month are free, and the count starts again on the 1st. Only a scan that found a bill counts, so a blurry photo costs nothing. Premium scans without a limit. Splitting, sending receipts and UPI QR codes are always free, however you enter the bill.

Marking a friend as paid

When a friend taps I've paid on their receipt and sends it back, opening it asks whether to tick them off. UPI doesn't tell SplitSnap that money moved, so check your bank or UPI app first. You can also tick a friend off yourself from the split.

Exporting your expenses

Settings → Export to a spreadsheet shares every split as a CSV file, with your share and what's still owed, for Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Export is part of Premium.

Restoring a purchase

Premium is tied to your Apple ID. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Cancel in the iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SplitSnap, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. If you started the yearly plan's free week, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your splits stay on your phone and are never uploaded, so they don't move to a new phone by themselves. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
