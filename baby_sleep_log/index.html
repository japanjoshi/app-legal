Questions

Good to know.

Is it a subscription?

No. After the free week, one payment of $9.99 keeps logging going for good. Nothing renews, and there is nothing to cancel.

What happens after the free week if I don't pay?

You can still end a sleep that's running, and view, edit, delete and export everything you've logged. Only logging new sleeps needs the one-time unlock.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. Your log stays on your iPhone and is included in your iCloud or computer backup like your other apps.

Does it predict naps or tell me what to do?

No. It records what happened and adds it up. You can set your own awake target if you like, but the app never suggests one. It's a log, not medical advice; talk to your pediatrician about your baby's sleep.

Can my partner use it too?

Family Sharing is on for the unlock, so a partner can unlock it on their own iPhone. Each phone keeps its own log.

Does it track feeds or diapers?

No, on purpose. It does one job, sleep, so the screen stays simple at night.
