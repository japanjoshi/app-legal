Baby Sleep Log: Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. Baby Sleep Log (on the App Store as Baby Sleep Tracker: Nap Log) is published by 1213 Studios. This page explains what the app keeps, where, and how to delete it.

The short version

There is no account. Your baby's sleep log stays on your iPhone. 1213 Studios never receives it and has no copy. The only data that leaves your phone is what Apple and RevenueCat need to check a purchase.

What the app stores on your iPhone

  • The sleeps you log: start and end times, and whether each was a nap or night sleep.
  • Your baby's first name, if you enter one. It is optional.
  • Your settings: theme, night window, night view, awake target and reminder choices, and which notices you have dismissed.
  • When your free week started, and whether the one-time unlock has been bought.

This is kept in an area of your iPhone shared only with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widget and Live Activity, so they can show and change the same log. It is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, like other app data, under your own Apple ID.

Purchases

The free week and the one-time unlock are handled by Apple's App Store. To check whether the unlock is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and the purchase history for this app. RevenueCat does not receive your sleep log, your baby's name or your payment details. 1213 Studios never sees your payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics services, and does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not ask for the advertising identifier.

Notifications

If you turn on the awake-target reminder, it is scheduled by your iPhone itself. Nothing about it is sent to us.

Exporting your data

You can export your whole log as a CSV file from History or Settings. Where that file goes is up to you, and it leaves the app only when you share it.

Deleting your data

Delete single sleeps from the log, or every sleep with Settings → Delete all sleeps. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. A copy may remain in an iCloud or computer backup until that backup is replaced. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to japanjoshi1234@gmail.com.

Children

The app is for parents and caregivers, not for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A baby's first name, if entered, stays on the parent's iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
