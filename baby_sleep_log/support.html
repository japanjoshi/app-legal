Baby Sleep Log: Support

Baby Sleep Log (on the App Store as Baby Sleep Tracker: Nap Log) is a one-tap sleep log for babies, published by 1213 Studios. If something isn't working, the answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? Email japanjoshi1234@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and the app version (Settings, near the bottom). We usually reply within two working days.

I tapped the button by mistake

Tap Undo on the message that appears for a few seconds. Later, tap the sleep in the list to change its times or delete it. A deleted sleep can be brought back with Undo too.

I forgot to tap "Woke up"

Tap "Woke up" now, then tap that sleep in the list and set the real time. Use the −5 / −1 / +1 / +5 buttons, or tap the time for the picker. If a nap runs over 4 hours or a night over 12, the app asks "Still asleep?" so you can fix it.

A sleep shows as a nap instead of the night (or the other way round)

Tap the sleep and switch Night / Nap. To change when the app counts sleep as night, go to Settings → Night starts and Night ends.

The screen went black

That's the night view. It shows while a night sleep runs in the dark theme, so the screen gives off as little light as possible. Tap anywhere to see everything; it comes back after a minute. Turn it off in Settings → Night view.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Baby Sleep Log. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize → Lock Screen, and add it under the clock. The widget starts and ends sleeps with one tap.

The awake-target reminder doesn't arrive

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Baby Sleep Log and make sure notifications are allowed. Check that a Focus mode (Sleep, Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing the app. The reminder is part of the one-time unlock once the free week is over.

Moving to a new phone

Your log is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, so restoring the backup on the new phone brings it with you. You can also export a CSV file from History or Settings at any time.

Restoring the unlock

The unlock is tied to your Apple ID. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all sleeps, or delete the app. Nothing is stored on our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Important

Baby Sleep Log is a log, not medical advice. If you're worried about your baby's sleep or health, talk to your pediatrician. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
