Bloop: Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Bloop is published by 1213 Studios.

What the app stores

What you enter in Bloop (your logs, Bristol types, colours, flags, notes, daily check-ins and answers during setup) is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere, including to us. The app has no accounts.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. To check whether a purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history for this app. We never see your payment details.

Usage stats and crash reports

To see which features get used and to fix bugs, the app sends anonymous usage stats and crash reports to Google, using Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. You can turn both off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats.

  • What is sent: which screens are opened and which buttons are used (for example "a log was saved" or "a report was made"), counts and timings (for example how many details were filled in), whether Bloop Plus is active, crash details (where in the app it failed), and device information such as the model, iOS version and app version. Google also works out an approximate region (country or city) from the connection, and assigns a random identifier to this installation of the app.
  • What is never sent: what you log. No Bristol types, colours, flags, notes, times of movements, check-in answers, or your answers during setup.
  • None of it is linked to your name, email or Apple ID, and it is not used for advertising. The app does not use your device's advertising identifier.
  • Google keeps usage stats for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days. See Firebase's privacy information.

Tracking

The app does not show ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Children

Bloop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes what it stored on your device. Usage stats and crash reports can't be traced back to you, so they expire on the schedule above. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
