Free week, plans and purchases

Bloop installed from version 1.1 starts with a free week. Once it ends, keeping Bloop needs a plan (weekly, or yearly where it's offered) or the lifetime unlock. All of them are sold through Apple's App Store and charged to your Apple ID.

  • Weekly and yearly plans: auto-renewing subscriptions. A new install is offered one of the two. If you start a plan with the free week, you are charged at the end of the free week, and then every week or every year, at the price shown before you confirm. A plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel or manage it any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription in Bloop's Settings. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current week or year stays active until it ends. The free week is offered once per Apple ID, and any unused part of it ends when you buy.
  • Lifetime unlock: a one-time purchase. It does not renew.
  • Bloop Plus: for anyone who started using Bloop before version 1.1, logging, history, check-ins, reminders and backups stay free, and Bloop Plus stays a one-time purchase that does not renew.

If a plan ends, the log on your phone is never deleted: you can still export it as a backup or a spreadsheet, or delete it. Purchases can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling from Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple's standard licence agreement also applies.
