Free logging, Bloop Plus and purchases

Logging, history, check-ins, reminders and backups in Bloop are free. Bloop Plus (Insights, the doctor report and Quick log) is a paid upgrade, sold through Apple's App Store and charged to your Apple ID.

  • Yearly plan: an auto-renewing subscription that starts with a free week where it's offered. If you start it with the free week, you are charged at the end of the free week, and then every year, at the price shown before you confirm. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel or manage it any time in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from Manage subscription in Bloop's Settings. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current year stays active until it ends. The free week is offered once per Apple ID, and any unused part of it ends when you buy.
  • Weekly plan: for anyone who started it in versions 1.1 to 1.3.0, the same terms, renewing every week.
  • Lifetime unlock: a one-time purchase. It does not renew.
  • Earlier one-time purchases: the one-time Bloop Plus sold until 15 November 2026 to people who started using Bloop before version 1.1 stays unlocked for good. It does not renew, and it is no longer sold.

If a plan ends, logging stays free and the log on your phone is never deleted: you can still export it as a backup or a spreadsheet, or delete it. Purchases can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling from Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
