My Jesus: Support

My Jesus answers what's on your heart with only the words Jesus said in the Gospels, word for word. It is published by 1213 Studios. The answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? Email japanjoshi1234@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and the app version (at the bottom of Settings). We usually reply within two working days.

The daily word isn't arriving

Open the iPhone Settings → Notifications → My Jesus and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Check that a Focus (Sleep, Work, Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing it. Open My Jesus once after changing the time zone or travelling; reminders are rescheduled each time the app opens.

Changing or turning off the reminder

In the app, open Settings and look under Daily word to pick a new time or turn it off.

Where do the answers come from?

Every answer is the words of Jesus from the World English Bible (or the King James Version with Plus), quoted exactly. Your question is matched to topics we prepared and reviewed. No AI writes anything. Tap the reference above any passage to read the whole chapter.

Sharing His words

Tap the share icon on any passage. With My Jesus Plus you can share an image for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Stories or a friend, as a Story or a Square, light or night. Sending the words as text is always free.

Saving an image to Photos

After tapping Share image, choose Save Image. If nothing is saved, open Settings → My Jesus → Photos and allow Add Photos Only.

Restoring My Jesus Plus

Plus is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Moving to a new phone

Your saved words, notes and questions stay on your phone and are never uploaded, so they don't move with you. Plus does: restore it as above.

If you are struggling

When a question mentions harming yourself, My Jesus shows help first. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number now. In the United States, call or text 988. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines.
