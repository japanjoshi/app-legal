My Jesus: Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. My Jesus is published by 1213 Studios.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type or keep in My Jesus stays on your device: the questions you ask, the topics you choose, the words you save, your notes and your reminder time. Questions are matched to passages on the phone itself; they are never sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no accounts, and 1213 Studios does not receive or hold what you enter.

Purchases

My Jesus Plus is a one-time purchase handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. To check whether you have it, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history for this app. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

When you share a passage, the image or text is made on your phone and goes only where you send it, through your phone's own share sheet. If you choose "Save Image", the app asks for permission to add the picture to your photo library. It cannot see or read your photos.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily word, the reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Analytics and advertising

The app shows no ads, uses no analytics or advertising services, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Help in a crisis

If you tap a helpline in the app, your phone opens its own call, message or browser app. My Jesus does not see or record that call or message.

Children

My Jesus is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Settings in the app can clear your saved words and recent questions. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
