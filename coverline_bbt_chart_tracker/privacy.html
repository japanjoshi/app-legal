Coverline: BBT Chart & Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Coverline: BBT Chart & Tracker is published by 1213 Studios.

What the app stores

Your temperatures, cycle dates, signs (period, cervical mucus, LH tests), tags and notes are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no server, and 1213 Studios does not receive or hold what you enter. If your device's own iCloud backup is on, this data is included in that backup, which Apple holds for you. Files you export (CSV, backup or PDF) go only where you send them.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. To check whether a purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase history for this app. We never see your payment details.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count anonymous usage, such as which screens are opened and whether a reading was saved. These events never contain health data: no temperatures, dates, period, mucus or LH results, notes, tags or cycle lengths. The app does not collect the advertising identifier, shows no ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

Reminders (the morning reminder if you turn it on, and one reminder before a free trial of Pro ends) are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to us.

Children

Coverline: BBT Chart & Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Settings › Your data › Delete all data, or deleting the app, removes what it stored on your device. To ask about purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to the address below.
