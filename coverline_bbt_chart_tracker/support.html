Coverline: Support

Coverline is a private basal body temperature (BBT) chart, published by 1213 Studios. The answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? Email japanjoshi1234@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and the app version (at the bottom of Settings). We usually reply within two working days.

The morning reminder isn't arriving

Open the iPhone Settings → Notifications → Coverline and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Check that a Focus (Sleep, Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing it. In the app, Settings → Morning reminder sets the time or turns it off.

How is the coverline suggested?

Once a temperature rises and stays up, Coverline uses the 3-over-6 rule: three readings above the highest of the six before them. It suggests a coverline just above those six, and you confirm it, move it, or say "Not yet". Readings marked as disturbed (poor sleep, illness, alcohol) are left out. Coverline does not predict fertile days and must not be used as contraception.

Fahrenheit or Celsius

Change it any time in Settings → Unit. Every reading is converted for you.

Restoring Coverline Pro

Pro is tied to your Apple ID. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Cancelling a subscription or free trial

Open the iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Coverline, at least 24 hours before it renews. Your charts, CSV export and backups stay free either way.

Moving to a new phone

Your charts are stored only on your phone. Before switching, open Settings → Your data → Back up to a file and save it (to Files, iCloud Drive or email). On the new phone, choose Restore from a backup on the same screen and pick that file.
